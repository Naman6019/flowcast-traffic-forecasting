**Verdict: the deep model did not clearly earn its added complexity.** The chosen LSTM scored a test RMSE of 54.27. That beats every classical model, so the PRD's benchmark is met. Against forest, the classical model chosen on validation (54.93), the gain is 0.67 vehicles per window, or 1.2%. The 95% interval for that gain, resampling whole test days, runs from 0.28 to 1.13, so it is measurably above zero. The best classical model on test was xgboost (54.62). A gain under 2% is counted here as too small to matter to operators, and the deep model costs 5.4x as much per prediction, needs its full window of history, and can't be read through a simple feature ranking. The LSTM, GRU and BiLSTM are all within 0.15 RMSE of each other, and accuracy barely falls from 30 to 120 minutes ahead, which suggests most of the remaining error is noise that no model can predict.
