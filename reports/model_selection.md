# Model selection

Generated by `python -m src.pipeline final`. Bold marks the model the rule chose.

**The rule (PRD 11.4).** Take the best validation score on the metric that matters for the task. A faster model is preferred if it is statistically tied with the best, meaning both its validation gap and its mean CV gap are within the standard error of the paired per-fold CV differences between the two. Pairing folds removes the spread that comes from which days a fold holds, which affects every model alike. Test scores are shown for the record and played no part in the choice.

**Why each metric.** Volume and travel time use RMSE because large misses at peak are what hurt operations. Congestion uses macro-F1 because Heavy and Severe are rare, and accuracy would reward ignoring them. Accident risk uses ROC-AUC to rank segments for patrols, with precision and recall reported at an operating threshold chosen for recall of at least 60%.

**What serves the dashboard.** Volume comes from the deep model (validation RMSE: classical 55.00, deep 53.09). Congestion comes from `regress_then_band_tuned`, compared using the production volume forecast:

- `regress_then_band`: validation macro-F1 0.7668
- `regress_then_band_tuned`: validation macro-F1 0.7680
- `xgboost`: validation macro-F1 0.7603
