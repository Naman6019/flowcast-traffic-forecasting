# Real-data validation: I-94 traffic

Generated by `python -m src.pipeline real`. The data is hourly westbound traffic on I-94 in Minnesota, from the UCI Metro Interstate Traffic Volume dataset (id 492). It's real sensor data, unlike the Northline set. Models were chosen on 2017 and scored on 6,287 hours in January to September 2018.

**What this tests:** volume only. This dataset has no speed, occupancy, capacity or accidents, so congestion, travel time and accident risk can't be validated here. It's also one road counted hourly rather than 25 roads every 30 minutes, so the two sets of numbers can't be compared directly.

## Result

The best model on validation, the GRU neural network, was off by 4.2% of traffic on average one hour ahead (95% interval 4.0 to 4.4), a test RMSE of 212 vehicles per hour. Its error is 74% lower than repeating the last hour, and 79% lower than using the same hour yesterday. The best classical model on validation, XGBoost, was close behind at 219, so as on Northline, the neural network's lead is small.

**This contradicts what the plan expected.** The plan assumed errors would be larger on real data, and they're about half Northline's in percentage terms. That doesn't mean real traffic is easier in general: this is one busy road counted hourly, averaging about 3,300 vehicles an hour, which smooths out much of the random variation that a 30-minute count on a smaller road shows.

**What this does and doesn't show.** The forecasting approach holds up on real, messy sensor data for volume. It says nothing about congestion, travel time or accident risk on real roads, because this dataset can't test them.

Forecasts run too high in snow (+88 vehicles), for the same reason they run too high in rain and fog on Northline: the model can't know the weather in the hour it's predicting. Conditions with fewer than 20 hours are left out of this table.

## Defects handled

| Defect | Entries |
|---|---:|
| Duplicate hours (one row per weather condition) | 7,629 |
| Hours absent from the log | 11,976 |
| Temperatures of 0 Kelvin | 10 |
| Impossible rainfall readings | 1 |
| Hours with a volume of zero | 2 |

Gaps of up to 2 hours are interpolated and never scored. Longer gaps stay blank, including one of about ten months in 2014 and 2015, and hours without enough history behind them aren't forecast.

## What was reused

Shared with the Northline pipeline: the quarantine ledger, short-gap filling, slot profile, chronological splits, time-series CV, tuning, model families, sequence models, conformal bands, metrics and selection rule. New for this dataset: the file adapter and a volume-only feature table, which follows the same design without the columns this dataset lacks.
